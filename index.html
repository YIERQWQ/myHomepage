Physical parameter identification from real-world videos

Built a cross-domain robust representation using dense depth and optical flow to infer material parameters from videos of elastoplastic bodies. Contributed to real-data processing, 2AFC physical-consistency scoring, and result visualization for a submission to a top computer vision conference.
